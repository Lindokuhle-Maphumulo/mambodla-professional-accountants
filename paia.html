02 · Purpose

Purpose of this PAIA Manual

This Manual is intended to help members of the public understand what records Mambodla holds, which records may be available without a formal request, how to make a request for access to a record, and how Mambodla processes personal information for purposes of POPIA.

In particular, the Manual provides the contact details of the Information Officer, describes the subjects on which Mambodla holds records and the categories of records held, identifies records available under other legislation, summarises the PAIA Guide and request process, and provides the POPIA information required by section 51 of PAIA.

04 · PAIA Guide

Guide on how to use PAIA

The Information Regulator has prepared and published a Guide in terms of section 10 of PAIA to assist people who wish to exercise rights under PAIA and POPIA. The Guide explains, among other things, how to request access to records, available remedies, applicable forms and fees, and the assistance available from the Regulator.

The Guide is available in the official languages and in accessible formats through the Information Regulator. It may be obtained from the Regulator’s website or requested from the Regulator.

Mambodla will provide reasonable assistance to a requester who needs help identifying the applicable PAIA process or prescribed form.

09 · Requesting access

How to request access to a record

A person requesting access to a record of Mambodla under section 50 of PAIA must show that the record is required for the exercise or protection of a right, comply with the procedural requirements of PAIA and the Regulations, and the request must not be subject to a lawful ground of refusal.

Use prescribed Form 2: Request for Access to Record.

Submit it to the Information Officer by email or at the physical/service address stated in this Manual. The form should identify the requester and requested record sufficiently, explain the right to be exercised or protected and why the record is required, state the preferred form of access, and provide any other information required by the prescribed form.

The prescribed PAIA forms are available from the Information Regulator. If a requester cannot complete the form because of illiteracy or disability, Mambodla will provide reasonable assistance as required by law.

10 · Fees & decisions

Fees, decision and grounds for refusal

PAIA permits prescribed request, reproduction, access and related fees to be charged in appropriate circumstances. Where a fee is payable, Mambodla will notify the requester in the prescribed manner before further processing or before access is provided, as applicable.

Mambodla will consider a properly submitted request and communicate its decision within the period prescribed by PAIA, subject to any lawful extension.

Access may be refused where PAIA permits or requires refusal, including to protect personal information of third parties, confidential commercial information, privileged records, safety or security interests, or other protected interests recognised by law.

11 · Complaints & remedies

Complaints and other remedies

There is no internal appeal procedure against the decision of the head of a private body under PAIA. A requester or affected third party may, subject to PAIA, lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator or approach a competent court.
