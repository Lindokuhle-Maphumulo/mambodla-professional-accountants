Privacy & Personal Information

Privacy Policy

Mambodla Professional Accountants respects privacy and aims to handle personal information responsibly, transparently and in accordance with applicable South African law.

Last updated: 23 September 2026

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy explains how Mambodla Professional Accountants ("Mambodla", "we", "us" or "our") handles personal information collected through this website, through website enquiries, and through related communications with prospective or existing clients.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect information such as your name, email address, telephone number, enquiry type, subject, message content and any other information you choose to provide.

Our hosting and delivery providers may also receive standard technical information generated when a website is accessed, such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps and diagnostic logs.

The Mambodla Individual Income Tax Calculator runs in your browser. The taxable income, age-band and medical-scheme details you enter into the calculator are not submitted to Mambodla by the calculator.

3. How we use personal information

  • To respond to enquiries and communicate with you.
  • To assess requests for accounting, tax, payroll or business advisory assistance.
  • To administer and deliver professional services where we have been engaged to do so.
  • To maintain appropriate business, security and compliance records.
  • To protect the website, our systems, our clients and our legitimate business interests.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory or professional obligations that apply to us.

4. Lawful processing

We process personal information only where there is a lawful basis to do so. Depending on the circumstances, this may include your consent, steps connected with a contract or requested service, compliance with a legal obligation, or legitimate interests recognised by the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

5. Service providers and disclosures

We may use trusted service providers to operate the website and deliver communications. The website contact form currently uses FormSubmit to transmit enquiries to Mambodla. The site may also use hosting, content-delivery, security and font-delivery services. Those providers may process limited information necessary to perform their services.

We may disclose information where required by law, regulation, court order, a competent authority, or where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

6. Cross-border processing

Some technology providers may process information outside South Africa. Where this occurs, we aim to use providers and arrangements that support appropriate protection of personal information in accordance with applicable law.

7. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide services, to resolve queries or disputes, and to meet applicable legal, regulatory, tax, accounting or professional record-keeping obligations.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, interference, misuse, alteration or disclosure. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can, however, be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask whether we hold personal information about you, request access to it, ask us to correct or delete information where appropriate, or object to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing already carried out lawfully.

If you believe your personal information has been handled in a way that does not comply with POPIA, you may raise the matter with us and you also have the right to approach the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

At the date of this policy, the Mambodla website does not intentionally use advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. Standard technical request information may still be processed by hosting or third-party delivery services required to operate the site.

11. Third-party websites

This website contains links to third-party services and social platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We encourage you to review those policies when leaving the Mambodla website.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services, technology or legal obligations change. The date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last updated.
